Auto-recognized conventions: labels Lit Len Lfr Lde, aliases Ait… (multiple values split on |), descriptions Dit…, sitelinks itwiki enwiki …, monolingual text Pxx_MONO_it (language in the header), the qid column, and qualifiers prefixed with S (attached to the statement they follow). Suffixes _STR _NUM _DATE _EDTF _GEO force a type; all other columns take the type you choose.
